# EduFlow AI

## Overview

EduFlow AI is an intelligent study planning platforms that helps the students organize, schedule, and track their learning goals effectively

## Problem Statement

Student often struggle to manage academic goals, exams, assignments,and learning objectives within limited time. Existing task management tools require manual planning and do not provide personalized study schedules.

## Solution

EduFlow AI provides an intelligent planning system that automatically generates study schedules based on goals, deadlines, workload and available study hours. The platform helps students stay organized, track progress and achieve their academic and career goals efficiently.

## Target Users

- College Students
- Placement Aspirants
- Self Learners
